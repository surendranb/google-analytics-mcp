Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 17, 2026 • Application: GA4 MCP (Google Analytics Model Context Protocol Server) • Maintainer: Surendran Balachandran (reachsuren@gmail.com)

At GA4 MCP (accessible at https://ga4mcp.com and via open-source package repositories), we believe privacy is an architectural guarantee, not merely a legal disclaimer. This Privacy Policy details how GA4 MCP accesses, processes, and protects your data when you interact with our application and connect your Google Analytics accounts.

Architecture Summary: GA4 MCP operates under a Zero-Knowledge, Zero-Server-Retention architecture. Your Google Analytics reporting data, property schemas, and OAuth refresh tokens are stored exclusively on your local workstation. Our edge gateway functions solely as an ephemeral OAuth authorization relay and never persists your tokens or queries.

1. Information We Access

When you choose to authenticate via 1-Click Google Sign-In, GA4 MCP requests the following permissions from your Google Account:

  • Google Analytics Read-Only Access (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/analytics.readonly): This sensitive scope permits your local AI agent (Claude Desktop, Cursor, Claude Code, Cline, etc.) to read Google Analytics 4 property metadata, report schemas, and traffic/conversion reports solely in response to queries you explicitly prompt.
  • Basic Profile & Email (openid, profile, email): Used solely to identify which Google account you have connected in your local environment display.

2. How Your Data Is Used

Your Google Analytics data is queried in real time directly from your machine:

  • Local Agent Query Execution: When you ask an AI model in your IDE (such as "What were my top referral channels this week?"), the local GA4 MCP server constructs a read query and transmits it directly to Google's official Analytics Data API.
  • Immediate In-Context Analysis: The returned data is returned directly into your local IDE or CLI session context so the AI model can synthesize an answer for you.

3. Google API Services User Data Policy Compliance (Limited Use Disclosure)

GA4 MCP's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Specifically:

  • No Third-Party Data Transfer or Sale: We do not transfer, distribute, or sell your Google user data to any external parties, data brokers, or advertisers under any circumstances.
  • No Advertising: We do not use or transfer your Google Analytics data to serve personalized, re-targeted, or interest-based advertisements.
  • No AI/ML Model Training: Your Google user data is never used to train, retrain, or fine-tune generalized machine learning models, foundational AI models, or public LLMs.
  • No Human Review: No human (including GA4 MCP maintainers or employees) has access to or reads your Google Analytics data, reports, or tokens. Data queries execute entirely programmatically between your local machine and Google.

4. Data Storage & Retention

  • Ephemeral Cloudflare Edge Gateway: During 1-Click Sign-in, Google redirects an authorization code to our gateway (ga4-gateway.reachsuren.workers.dev or custom domain). The gateway executes an atomic exchange with Google's OAuth endpoint, returns the resulting token bundle directly to your local loopback server (http://127.0.0.1:18443), and immediately discards the tokens from memory. The gateway maintains zero persistent databases and does not log credentials.
  • Local Workstation Storage: Your authentication credentials reside exclusively on your local filesystem at ~/.config/ga4-mcp/vault.json protected with strict Unix user permissions (chmod 0600).
  • Direct API Communication: All subsequent Google Analytics Data API and Admin API calls are made directly from your workstation to https://analyticsdata.googleapis.com. Your analytics data never transits our gateway servers.

5. Alternative Local Service Account Authentication

If you prefer not to use OAuth, GA4 MCP fully supports 100% offline authentication using your own Google Cloud Service Account key (GOOGLE_APPLICATION_CREDENTIALS) or Google Cloud Application Default Credentials (ADC). In this mode, no network connection to our OAuth gateway is ever initiated.

6. Data Revocation & Deletion

You have absolute control over your authentication at all times:

  • Revoke via Google Account: You can revoke GA4 MCP's access at any time by visiting your Google Account Permissions Settings and clicking "Remove Access".
  • Delete Local Tokens: You can delete all local session and credential files at any time by running:
    rm -rf ~/.config/ga4-mcp
